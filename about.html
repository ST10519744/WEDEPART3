Our Story: A Pet Store Run by Veterinary Nurses

Whisker & Wag started in 2018 with two former veterinary nurses who were tired of watching pet owners get bad advice from staff who had never worked with animals. They opened a small pet store in Hatfield where customers could ask real questions and get real answers. A few years and a lot of loyal customers later, we opened a second branch in Centurion and added an in-store grooming salon. We're still an independent pet store and a small, family-run business, just one that knows a lot about pets.

Explore our grooming and pet supplies or visit a branch.

Mission

Help pet owners make good decisions for their animals through honest advice and quality products, not just a sale.

Vision

To be the pet store people in Gauteng trust more than the big chains, because we know what we're talking about.

Meet the Team

Our team includes two qualified veterinary nurses, a dog groomer with over ten years of experience, and a small but dedicated crew who all have pets of their own at home. Have a question? Ask us here.
